Savor | Savor-bonbon

2026, Branded content, Social Launch, Motion, Graphics

Savor is a food-tech company developing innovative, sustainable alternatives to traditional cooking oils, using carbon dioxide and hydrogen to create fats and oils without relying on conventional agriculture.
As part of the creative team, I was responsible for bringing the Savor project to life across multiple touchpoints. My work included branded content creation, case study development, project launch, and social media launch, translating the project into a coherent visual and communication system across digital channels.

For Savor, we wanted the butter to feel as real and indulgent as it is. We focused on capturing its rich, tactile texture through the visuals, turning sustainability into something sensory and desirable.
This sensorial quality carries through the overall web experience, expressed through smooth motion, a refined visual language and carefully crafted details — creating a digital experience that feels as rich and considered as the product itself.
